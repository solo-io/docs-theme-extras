That is the whole point of the refactor, so it is worth stating why the four previous strategies are gone. Hugo does not expose whether a shortcode was called as {{%% %%}} or {{< >}}. The old templates therefore GUESSED the form from the shape of `.Inner` — six regex heuristics in utils/inner-shape.html — and picked one of four emit strategies. Every leak in solo-io/docs#3280 is either a misfired guess or a DOUBLE RENDER: content that RenderString had already turned into HTML being handed back to Goldmark to parse again. Emitting raw makes the whole class structurally impossible. Verified against hugo v0.160.1: at top level, `.Inner` is raw markdown in BOTH forms, and a percent-form gate that raw-emits produces output byte-identical to the same body with the tags deleted. THE CONTRACT THIS DEPENDS ON: the gate must be in PERCENT form, so its output re-enters the markdown stream. Angle form substitutes after Goldmark has run, so raw markdown would survive as literal text. Two mechanisms keep that true: - reuse.html and rebase.html normalize angle -> percent before rendering (search GATE-FORM-NORMALIZATION-v1); - `tests/gate-form.spec.ts` fails the build on an angle-form gate authored directly in content/.
