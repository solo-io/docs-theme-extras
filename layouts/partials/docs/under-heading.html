` and below the release badges (`page-badges`), if any. DEFAULT: nothing. HOW THIS DIFFERS FROM `after-title.html`, which is the only reason both exist. The title area is a flex container with two children: the heading column on the left, the copy/context buttons on the right. `after-title` renders AFTER that whole container closes; this slot renders INSIDE its left child. On a wide viewport the two look almost the same — both land under the heading — and the difference only shows up in two places: - NARROW VIEWPORTS. The container is `flex-col` below Hextra's `sm` breakpoint, so the buttons stack UNDER the heading. Content in `after-title` therefore falls BELOW the buttons; content in this slot stays directly under the heading, above them. - VERTICAL CENTRING. At `sm` and up the container is `items-center`, so anything added here makes the left column taller and the buttons re-centre against it. `after-title` cannot move the buttons. Pick this one for a per-page marker that belongs to the TITLE (a status chip, a "verified" badge). Pick `after-title` for something that belongs to the PAGE and should span its full width — that slot is not constrained by the heading column's width. RENDERED ON DETAIL PAGES ONLY (`docs/single.html`), and only when the page has a title at all — it sits inside the `if .Title` branch, unlike the other two prose-column slots, which run regardless.
